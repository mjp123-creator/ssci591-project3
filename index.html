LA Metro Rail Stations

About this map: Explore Los Angeles rail stations and click a point to see its station name and line. The stations load directly from the public LA GeoHub rail station service each time the page opens. I adapted Esri's Intro to FeatureLayer sample by changing the location, basemap, symbols, title, and popup fields. The legend explains the line colors, and the Home button returns to the starting view.

Course demonstration, not a trip planner. The source retains historical line names and was last edited in 2017; live access does not guarantee current transit information.
